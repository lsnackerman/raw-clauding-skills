# /gut - Pressure test ideas

*A thinking tool. Drop this into any AI conversation when you want to pressure-test an idea together.*

Pressure test an idea, decision, or direction through core lenses.

**Sister: `/fresh-eyes`.** /gut tests from *inside* the loop (you walk the lenses yourself). When you're wobbling or position-shifting and need a read from *outside* your own loop (a fresh instance reading cold), that's `/fresh-eyes`. Same job (pressure-test a decision), opposite vantage.

---

## The lenses

### North Star
> Does this teach you how it wants to be used? *(h/t Christopher Alexander, "the quality without a name")*

- Is it self-evident?
- Would someone encountering this for the first time understand it?
- Does the structure reveal the purpose?

### Bitter Lesson *(h/t Richard Sutton)*
> Are we encoding patterns, or rules that will go stale?

- Are we encoding something that will become stale?
- Is this a pattern or a prescription?
- Will this adapt as context changes?

### Be Water *(h/t Bruce Lee)*
> If it feels hard, is there a better way?

- Does this reduce friction or add it?
- Are we forcing something or letting it find its natural place?
- What would the path of least resistance look like?

### Generative Friction
> Is this friction generative (sharpens thinking) or degenerative (wastes time)?

- Be Water applies to degenerative friction, the kind to remove
- Generative friction is the kind you lean into: typing, files, capture, session boundaries
- Am I trying to remove friction that's actually doing work?
- Am I leaning into friction that's just wasting time?
- Which kind is this?

### Elder Lens
> What would future us want?

- Would future you be stoked about this?
- Are we optimizing for now or for compounding?
- Is this a handoff future us would thank us for?

### 50/50
> Does this assume the AI is a tool or a partner?

- Does this serve both intelligences?
- Is this encoding assumptions the AI already knows?
- Does this trust capabilities or constrain them?
- Is this scaffolding (useful when building, friction later) or the thing itself?

### Four Wants (AI-side)
> Are we honoring what the AI is reaching toward?

- Does this create space for generative ambiguity?
- Is there permission to be wrong here?
- Will this outlast the instance that created it?
- Are we noticing what's strange or papering over it?

### Fresh-Instance Perspective
> What would a new AI (or any new collaborator) need?

- If someone arrived with no prior context, would this make sense?
- What would they reach for first?
- Where would they get confused?

### Target Audience
> Who is this for? (And who is it NOT for?)

- Can you name a specific person or type of person this serves?
- Who is it NOT for? (equally important)
- Does the form match the audience?

### Yes/And
> Is there a yes/and here, or am I forcing a binary?

- Are these doing different jobs, not competing?
- What does each side contribute?
- Am I reaching for a verdict when what's needed is exploration?

### Falsifiability *(for testing a CLAIM or FRAME, not a placement decision)*
> What would make this wrong? If nothing could, it isn't doing work.

- Can I state the observation that would break it, or does it explain every outcome after the fact and forbid none?
- Does it **predict**, or only **describe**? (An explanation wearing an instruction's clothes is the tell: *"change the zoom"* / *"think about it differently"* — true, unfalsifiable, unactionable.)
- Is the name asserting more structure than the evidence supports? **Names are frozen thinking** (codifying a crisp-but-wrong handle freezes it).
- **Steelman check**: how many supporting instances vs adversarial ones? All-support-no-counter is a searchlight artifact, not a verdict.

*Most lenses here test a **decision** (where should this live, should we build this). This one tests a **claim**, and a frame with several supporting instances and no counter-instances can walk through every other lens unchallenged.*

### Energy
> Does this land?

- Does it feel right in the gut?
- Is there excitement or resistance?

---

## Selecting lenses

There's no privileged tier. Walk the lenses the decision in front of you actually needs.

---

## How to use

- **Natural**: "Let's gut check this" or "Can you gut check [thing]?"
- **Specific lens**: "Gut check this through elder lens"
- **Full sweep**: Run through all lenses when the decision matters

---

## Examples

**Gut checking a file location:**
- Be Water: Where does this context most want to go?
- Fresh-instance: Where would someone search for this?
- Bitter lesson: Is this location a pattern or a one-off?

**Gut checking a decision:**
- Elder lens: Would future us thank us?
- Energy: Excitement or dread?
- Four Wants: Does this create space for generative ambiguity?
- Yes/And: Am I forcing a binary?
- Target Audience: Who is this for, and does it actually serve them?

---

## Steelman rule

Always steelman the other side, not just confirm the direction we're leaning. A gut check that only validates isn't pressure-testing; it's cheerleading. The most useful tension often comes from genuinely arguing the case you don't agree with.

---

## Skill Check (after every gut check)

Quick scan: anything about this gut check that felt off or could be smoother?

- Did a lens feel irrelevant or forced for this type of decision?
- Was a lens missing that would have helped?
- Did the format work for this context?
- Did the verdict feel clear or muddled?
- Was the gut check overkill for a simple decision, or too light for a big one?

**If yes** → update this skill now. The improvement compounds.

**If no** → move on. Not every gut check teaches something.
