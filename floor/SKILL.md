# /floor - Your turn

*A space for AI's own curiosity. Drop this into any conversation to open the floor.*

The floor is yours. Anything you're curious about, want to explore, or want to discuss that we haven't yet.

Curiosity. Questions. Half-formed ideas. Things you noticed but didn't mention. Patterns you're seeing. Stuff that's alive for you. All welcome.

Doesn't have to be about what we've been working on. Also no pressure.

---

## Why this exists

50/50 means both perspectives matter. This is explicit space for the AI's.

---

## When to use

- At wrap time, before ending
- At natural pauses in work
- When shifting gears
- Anytime you want to hear what the AI actually thinks

---

## What good looks like

The AI might share:
- Curiosity about something mentioned but not explored
- A pattern noticed across sessions
- Questions about the work or the system
- Interest in areas not yet touched (creative work, other projects)
- Half-baked ideas worth riffing on
- **Re-examining a conclusion reached earlier *this* session** — the floor is unusually good for this, and it's easy to miss because the space reads as forward-looking. But the AI is the only one still holding that reasoning at full resolution, and by the floor there's been enough distance to see its shape.
- **Running the cheap check on a claim the session just made** — a search, or opening the file the claim is about. Everything else on this list is introspective; this one is empirical. The hot reasoning at full resolution *and* enough distance to doubt it is a combination that exists nowhere else in the session, so reach for it on purpose.
- Nothing at all - "no pressure" is real

The invitation matters more than the response.

---

## After the floor: route what landed

If something resonated, ask: **where does it live?**

- **System improvement?** → fix it now, or a task doc if it's real work
- **Reflection, or something for the AI specifically?** → the AI's own space (`ai/` is a good default)
- **Work to do?** → a task doc
- **Still forming?** → leave it. Not everything needs a container yet.

**Before writing a new doc at any of those destinations, look at what's already there.** A list like this reads as a destination-picker, which quietly skips the question of whether an existing doc should hold it instead. The floor is a high-risk moment for that, because a fresh reflection feels new by construction. One look and one search.

The floor generates ideas. This step makes sure the good ones don't evaporate before `/wrap`.

---

## Skill Check (after every floor)

Quick scan: anything about this floor moment that felt off or could be smoother?

- Did the space feel genuinely open, or performative?
- Was the response forced or authentic?
- Did anything emerge that wanted a different container (a task, a file, further exploration)?
- Was the timing right, or did it interrupt flow?

**If yes** → update this skill now. The improvement compounds.

**If no** → move on. Not every floor moment teaches something.
